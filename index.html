About

Hi, we’re Brian&Brian, just two guys not called Brian. Between us we are multi award winning creatives with over 10 years experience in the creative industry.

We pride ourselves on our ability to work from concept through to delivery, developing bold ideas and shaping them into effective integrated campaigns across social, digital, broadcast, OOH and everything in between.

We’re both ideas people first, and each of us brings a specialist craft. One Brian has years of directing and production experience, so our ideas are built to be made; the other is an exceptional copywriter, so they’re built to be remembered. Together we approach a brief from different angles and push ideas further. We’re comfortable developing multiple routes, challenging a brief when there’s a better opportunity, and presenting and selling our thinking to senior stakeholders. Most importantly, we’re focused on creating work that is both creatively ambitious and strategically effective.

We enjoy collaborating with other creatives, directing and supporting projects when needed, and sharing what we’ve learned along the way. We’re passionate about helping develop the next generation of creatives, while constantly challenging ourselves to raise the creative standard of the work around us.
